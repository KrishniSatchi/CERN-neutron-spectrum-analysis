# Sources

## h10_neutron_coefficients.csv
Neutron fluence-to-ambient-dose-equivalent coefficients H*(10)/Phi in pSv cm^2,
transcribed by hand from:

- ICRP Publication 74 (1996), Conversion Coefficients for use in Radiological
  Protection against External Radiation, Annals of the ICRP 26(3-4),
  Table A.42 (printed page 200).

Cross-check at 13 energies against:

- E. S. da Fonseca and W. W. Pereira, "New Quantities (Hp and H*) in Neutron
  Monitor Calibration", BIPM working document CCRI(III)/01-10, Table 2.
